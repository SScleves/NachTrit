# Nachtrit: 25 levels

The story, the difficulty curve and the content of every level. Content comes from the vault
at https://github.com/SScleves/dutch-lessons (`_site/words`, `_site/situations/*`,
`_site/maps/H01..H18`, `_site/maps/MOC *`, `Dutch Lessons/02 - Grammar`).

## The story (intro, pixel art, skippable)

Six panels, big pixels, typed text, one joke per panel.

1. Night over the polder. A windmill turns backwards. "Something came in with the mist."
2. Empty streets, a bakfiets on its side. "Everyone who could speak Dutch is gone. Or changed."
3. One man in a kitchen with two dogs and a half-read grammar book. "One man is left to deliver what the town needs."
4. He opens the book. Page one: de or het. "He does not speak Dutch."
5. The dogs look at him. "..."
6. He puts on the helmet. "Fak. Oké. Nachtrit."

Then the first mission card. Every mission card is three lines: where you go, what you carry,
what the dead want from you there.

## How difficulty moves

Five acts of five levels. Each act changes the game itself, not only the words.

| Act | Levels | Content | Lanes | Obstacles | Forks per delivery | Hint | Speed | Lamp decay |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Thuisstraat | 1-5 | single words, pictures and English | 2 | none, then puddles | 1 | word shown with its article after each fork | slow, no ramp | very slow |
| 2 De wijk | 6-10 | words with de/het, numbers, fixed phrases | 3 | fences, slow | 1 | first letter of the answer | slow ramp | slow |
| 3 Het dorp | 11-15 | short sentences, one gap: verb forms, V2, niet/geen, questions | 3 | fences, carts | 1-2 | none, but the why after every fork | medium | medium |
| 4 Kantoor en baan | 16-20 | past, future, separable verbs, word order | 3 | all, denser | 2-3 | none | fast | medium |
| 5 De lange nacht | 21-25 | sub-clauses, die/dat, imperfectum, reflexives, softeners, reading forks with subtle meaning | 3 | all, dense | 3-4 | none | fast, ramps | fast, follower visible |

Other rules of the curve:

- A level is 6 deliveries. Pass with 4 of 6 right. Three stars for 6 of 6 with the lamp never below half.
- Every level replays words from the two levels before it. Nothing is taught once and dropped.
- Mistakes in a level go to the vault export, and come back in the next level's filler forks.
- Reading forks (Dutch on the board, English on the lanes) start in act 2 with greetings and
  prices, and carry the subtle ones (moet niet, wel, al twee jaar) only in act 5.
- Endless mode (what exists today) unlocks after level 10 and uses everything unlocked so far.

## Act 1: Thuisstraat (levels 1-5)

Single words. Two lanes, so every fork is a coin flip with a clue. The road is your own street.
Source: `H01 — Welkom`, `MOC Getallen`, `MOC Lidwoorden & voornaamwoorden`, the vocab notes
hond, huis, vrouw, tuin, buur.

| # | Mission | Content | What's new |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | De honden. Walk the dogs to the end of the street and back. Something follows. | 10 words from your house: de hond, het huis, de vrouw, de tuin, de deur, de straat, de fiets, de lamp, de nacht, de mist. English prompt, Dutch on the signs. | Lanes, steering, the lamp. No obstacles. The word is spoken when you hit it. |
| 2 | De buren. Bring the neighbours a candle. They only answer in Dutch. | Greetings: hallo, goedemorgen, goedenavond, dag, doei, dank je wel, alsjeblieft, sorry, ja, nee. | First reading fork: a Dutch greeting on the board, pick the English. |
| 3 | De brievenbus. Post letters to 10 houses. The numbers are the only thing left on the doors. | nul to twaalf. Sign shows the digit, lanes show the Dutch word. | Puddles appear. Wisps appear and give lamp. |
| 4 | Ik ben. Say who you are to the ghost at the door. | ik, jij, hij, zij, wij, jullie + ben, bent, is, zijn. Fork: "we ___" lanes: zijn / ben. | Two-word forks: the gap is in a tiny sentence for the first time. |
| 5 | Nachtwinkel. The corner shop at 3 AM. Buy milk, bread, eggs. | Shop words: de melk, het brood, het ei, de kaas, de appel, het water, de koffie, de thee, het bier, de wijn. Prices in numbers 13-20. | First boss: the follower appears in the mirror on the summary card if you got 2 wrong in a row. Level pass needs 4 of 6. |

## Act 2: De wijk (levels 6-10)

Three lanes. Every noun comes with its article now, and the article is a fork of its own.
Numbers go to 100, fixed phrases arrive as whole chunks. Obstacles start.
Source: `_site/situations/supermarket`, `butcher`, `takeaway`, `polite-phrases`, `MOC Getallen`,
`MOC Tijd, dagen & maanden`, grammar note `de vs het`.

| # | Mission | Content | What's new |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | De supermarkt. The shelves whisper. Find bread, pay, leave. | 10 supermarket phrases as chunks: Pardon, waar vind ik het brood? Kan ik pinnen? Wilt u een tasje erbij? Dat was het, dank u wel. Fork picks the missing word of the phrase. | Third lane. Fences. Reading forks for shop replies: In gangpad drie, achteraan. |
| 7 | De slager. The butcher is dead and still open. Order 200 grams of something you can name. | Butcher phrases, weights and numbers 20-100: twintig, dertig, honderd, twee ons, een pond. | Number forks show the digit, lanes show Dutch words with near misses (veertien / veertig). |
| 8 | De of het. Deliver labels to every object in the ruined house. | de/het gates for 30 nouns from levels 1-7 plus H01. Rule of thumb cards from the vault note: plurals de, diminutives het. | Pure de/het run. Fast forks, short gaps. |
| 9 | Afhalen. Order a pizza by phone from a restaurant nobody has seen since the mist. | Takeaway and polite phrases: Mag ik twee broden, alstublieft? Ik wil graag... Alstublieft / alsjeblieft. Days: maandag to zondag. | Phrase chunks with two gaps in a row. |
| 10 | De kalender. The town clock stopped. Set the days and months back. | Days, months, time words: vandaag, morgen, gisteren, vanavond, 's ochtends. | End of act: a long mixed run. Unlocks endless mode. |

## Act 3: Het dorp (levels 11-15)

Short real sentences with one gap. Verb forms, V2, negation, questions, modal verbs.
Source: `MOC Kernwerkwoorden`, `MOC Top werkwoorden`, `03 - Verbs` tables, `MOC Vraagwoorden`,
`MOC Ontkenning`, `_site/situations/directions`, `meeting-people`, `small-talk`, grammar notes
presens, V2 word order, niet vs geen, modale werkwoorden.

| # | Mission | Content | What's new |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | Werkwoorden. The road is a conjugation table. Fill the gaps or the lamp goes. | presens of hebben, zijn, gaan, komen, doen, wonen, werken: ik werk / jij werkt / wij werken. | The sentence card shows the subject, the forks show the forms. Carts appear. |
| 12 | De weg kwijt. Every street sign points the wrong way. Ask the dead for directions. | Directions situation: Waar is...? rechtdoor, links, rechts, bij het stoplicht. Question words: wie, wat, waar, wanneer, hoe, waarom. | Questions put the verb first: fork between "Waar woon jij" and "Waar jij woont". |
| 13 | Nieuwe mensen. Meet the three survivors in the church. Introduce yourself. | meeting-people situation: Ik ben..., Ik kom uit..., Ik woon in..., Aangenaam. Numbers for age. | V2 forks: "Morgen ___" with "ga ik" vs "ik ga". |
| 14 | Niet of geen. The survivors ask for things you don't have. | niet vs geen in 15 sentences: geen tijd, niet duur, niet naar kantoor, nog niet. | Reading forks with negation: Ik heb niets gezien. |
| 15 | Mag ik? The gate out of the village has a keeper. Ask properly. | Modal verbs + infinitive at the end: Mag ik..., Kan ik..., Ik moet..., Zou je... kunnen...? The moet niet / mag niet trap first appears here as a reading fork. | Two forks per sentence: modal, then infinitive at the end. |

## Act 4: Kantoor en baan (levels 16-20)

Your own world: the standup, the golf course, the home office. The past and the future.
Source: `04 - Phrases & Scenarios` (De standup, Koffiepraatje, Op de golfbaan, Uitleggen wat je
doet), `_site/situations/office`, grammar notes perfectum, zijn of hebben, 't kofschip,
scheidbare werkwoorden, futurum, tijd-manier-plaats, aan het.

| # | Mission | Content | What's new |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | De standup. Your team is dead and still holds the standup at 9. Report what you did yesterday. | Perfectum with hebben: gewerkt, gespeeld, gebouwd, gebeld. 't kofschip: -t or -d. | Participle forks at the end of the sentence. Speed up. |
| 17 | Naar de golfbaan. The course is open, the players are not alive. | zijn vs hebben: Ik ben naar de golfbaan gegaan. Ik heb gegolft. Golf scenario phrases. | Auxiliary fork, then participle fork. Obstacles denser. |
| 18 | Uitlaten. The dogs again, but now you say what you did with them. | Separable verbs in all four positions: laat uit, liet uit, uitgelaten, moet uitlaten. Also opbellen, afspreken, meegaan, langskomen. | Forks where the prefix must land at the end. |
| 19 | Morgen. The survivors plan an escape. Say what you are going to do. | gaan + infinitive, zullen for promises, present + time word as future. Time-manner-place. | Long sentence builds: 3 forks, including an order fork (zaterdag met Mark naar de golfbaan). |
| 20 | Aan het werk. The home office at night. Say what is happening right now. | zijn + aan het + infinitive. Mixed act 4 review. | End of act: the follower is visible in the fog behind you on the summary card. |

## Act 5: De lange nacht (levels 21-25)

Sub-clauses, relative pronouns, the simple past, reflexives, softeners. Then everything.
Source: grammar notes bijzin, die vs dat, imperfectum, reflexieve werkwoorden, zachtmakers,
`_site/situations/opinions`, `compliments`, `sweet-words`, `visiting`, `H17 — Thuis`, `H18 — Bij de politie`.

| # | Mission | Content | What's new |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21 | Omdat. The dead want reasons. Explain yourself with omdat, als, dat. | bijzin: verb to the end. want vs omdat. Als first, then inversion. | Forks with whole clauses on the signs. Lane strip shows them in full. |
| 22 | Die of dat. Describe the survivors and what they carry. | die vs dat for de/het words, verb to the end of the relative clause. | Fast de/het recall from act 2 decides the fork. |
| 23 | Vroeger. The oldest ghost tells the story of the night the mist came. You finish her sentences. | Imperfectum: woonde, werkte, duurde, regende, was, had, ging, kwam. | Story mode: a long sentence chain, 4 forks, no filler between them. |
| 24 | Bij de politie. The last living officer takes your statement. Reflexives and softeners. | zich voelen, zich vergissen, zich herinneren, zich voorstellen. even, wel, maar, hoor. H18 phrases. | Reading forks with the subtle meanings: Het gaat wel. Ik doe het wel. Je moet niet gaan. |
| 25 | Dageraad. Ride to the sea before sunrise with everything you know. | All rules, all situations, the hardest reading forks, your own logged mistakes as the final forks. | The follower is on the road. Lamp decays fast. Sunrise at the end if you make it. |

## How the game itself changes along the way

- Lanes: 2 in act 1, 3 from act 2.
- Signs: single words in act 1-2, chunks in act 2, clauses from act 4. Board size grows with text.
- Hints: full hint in act 1 (the right word glows faintly), first letter in act 2, none after.
- Speed: fixed in act 1, ramps per act. Act 5 ramps within the level.
- Lamp: decays slower early. Correct answers give more lamp early, less later.
- Obstacles: none, puddles, fences, carts, then dense.
- Forks per delivery: 1, 1, 1-2, 2-3, 3-4.
- The follower: a rumour in act 1, a mirror glimpse in act 2, a shape in the fog in act 4, on the road in act 5.
- Scenery per act: your street, the shops, the village and church, office and golf course, the dyke to the sea.

## Build order

1. Level data format: a level has a mission card, a content list, fork settings, and game settings.
2. Levels 1-5 with the word fork type (English or picture prompt, Dutch on the signs) and 2 lanes.
3. The pixel intro.
4. Level select screen with acts, stars, and locked levels.
5. Acts 2-3 content, generated from the vault situation files by a script.
6. Acts 4-5, mostly from the sentence set that already exists in `data.js`.

## CEFR and node types (from the Duolingo structure Santi shared)

- Acts 1-2 are A1: single words, then phrases, sentences of at most 6 words, present tense of zijn, hebben, willen, no inversion before level 13.
- Acts 3-4 are A2: at most 10 words, perfectum, modals, V2 inversion, separable verbs in the present.
- Act 5 is B1: at most 15 words, imperfectum, sub-clauses, reflexives, zou + infinitive.
- Node types per district, later: core places (star), a practice ride built from your logged mistakes (dumbbell), a story ride with one long passenger (book), and a trophy ride that reviews the whole district before the next one opens.
- Fork kinds now: word (English prompt, Dutch signs), reading (Dutch board, English signs), listening (you hear the Dutch, Dutch signs), build (gaps in a sentence).
- The Duolingo content-engine prompt is the brief for Opus agents that write later districts, with `levels.js` items as the required output shape.

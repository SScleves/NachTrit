# Nachtrit

**Play it now, no install:** https://sscleves.github.io/NachTrit/ (any phone or computer browser; arrow keys or swipe to change lane, Enter or tap to go in).

**Every town open, to look at the art:** https://sscleves.github.io/NachTrit/?unlock=1 (needed once per device, the browser remembers it; https://sscleves.github.io/NachTrit/?unlock=0 locks the towns again; stars and progress are not touched).

Source and plan: https://github.com/SScleves/NachTrit. Working instructions for the heavy-lifting agent: `Opus 5.5 DT/README.md`.

Horror arcade runner for Dutch grammar. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

    python3 serve.py

then open http://localhost:8765 (8080 is qBittorrent on this machine) on your phone or laptop.

Concept and rules of the build: `CONCEPT.md`. Sentences: `data.js`.

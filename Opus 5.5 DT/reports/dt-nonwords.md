# Non-words in wrong options (PLAN 3c.2)

Generated by `node qa/audit-distractors.mjs`. Full data: `dt-nonwords.json` in this folder. (The October distractor audit is `dt-audit-distractors.md`; this file does not replace it.)

Every option of every build fork and every `wrong` of a word item is split into words and checked against the OpenTaal list (`dictionary-nl`, read with `nspell`) plus `qa/allow-words.txt`. Right answers and sentence text may also be compounds of listed words (kaas + boer); a wrong option must be in the list itself, because a split like gesp + eelt would let gespeelt pass.

Policy (PLAN 3c): a form that does not exist may only appear in a **Spelling** fork: 't kofschip, open and closed syllables (which covers the adjective -e and plural spelling), plural and diminutive spelling. Everything else is replaced by a real Dutch form that is wrong in the sentence.

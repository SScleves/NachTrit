# Fork audit (PLAN 3b.1)

Generated by `node qa/audit-forks.mjs`. Full data: `dt-audit-forks.json` in this folder.

Every build fork is compared option by option with its right answer. A wrong option can differ in **word order**, **form** of the same word (werkt / werkte, groot / grote, ben / is, an extra te), **word choice** (deze / dit, met / aan, an extra or missing small word) or **spelling** (not a Dutch word, checked against the OpenTaal list). A fork is **mixed** when its wrong options together differ in more than one of these, so the player cannot tell what the fork asks.

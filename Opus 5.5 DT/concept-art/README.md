# Santi's concept art: the target for everything

These are the pictures Santi shared. The whole project aims to look like them. Read
CONCEPT-ART.md next to them (it describes each one, and image B, the zombie canal at night, which
was lost before it could be saved; its description is the only record).

- A-foggy-village.png: the colour key. Blue-grey haze, dark gabled houses, amber windows, wet road
  with lamp reflections, canal, drawbridge, windmill and spire fading into fog.
- C-concept-sheet.png: the cast and props sheet. Zombie silhouettes (tall, thin, hunched, long
  arms, glowing yellow eyes) and variants (bucket hat, bonnet, cheese carrier, on a bike), bikes,
  cheese, clogs, lanterns, bollards, fingerposts, gabled canal houses.
- D-foggy-village-with-zombies.png: A with the dead. THE frame the 3D game is judged against:
  a zombie peering over a fence by a lit window, shamblers under the lamp cones, a bike on the
  fence, the small red figure, the drawbridge, continuous terraced houses, trees, skyline.
- E-dorp-gameplay-sheet.png: the gameplay sheet. Words as warm glowing light above the wet road,
  run / jump / slide / swerve / collect / survive, power-ups (slow motion, shield, magnet, double
  points), hazards (zombies, bikes, canal edges, trams and cars), environments (town streets,
  canals and bridges, markets, countryside), the word-learned card with icon, meaning and speaker.
- F-map-concept.png: the world map. A raised night diorama of the Netherlands in dark water,
  dense silhouettes (mills, spires, lighthouses, boats, cows, tulip fields), one glowing landmark
  vignette per town, a dashed amber route with lamp dots, lock badges, number chips, names in
  tracked capitals with the CEFR band, a cyclist at the current town, NEDERLAND title, footer
  controls, the Endless night button. (Home town in the game is Alblasserdam, not Rotterdam.)

Measured from the PNGs: mean luma A 64, D 52, sheets 35; sky in D about rgb(64,73,84) at the
top and rgb(50,56,63) at the horizon. Cold, muted, low contrast far away; saturated only in amber
windows, the orange hero and yellow zombie eyes.

# Concept art references (from Santi)

Santi shared four concept images. The image files were lost when the laptop rebooted; if PNGs
appear in this folder (reference/*.png), study them first. Until then, this is a faithful
description of each, written from the originals. Everything here is a target for the intro art,
the level scenes and the 3D game.

## A. The foggy village (INSIDE mood, the colour key for the whole game)

A misty Dutch village street at dusk, seen from pavement height, almost monochrome blue-grey.

- Left: dark brick houses with steep gabled roofs and chimneys, a picket fence and a low hedge.
  A few windows glow warm amber; their light is the only warmth in the frame. Tall, slim, modern
  street lamps with flat heads stand along the pavement.
- A small figure in a red top walks on the wet pavement; his reflection shows in a puddle.
- The road runs straight into the fog with a white dashed centre line and a raised kerb; a parked
  car is a faint silhouette far away. The asphalt is wet and catches light.
- Right: a canal with tall reeds and grasses in the foreground, wooden mooring posts, a wooden
  drawbridge (ophaalbrug) with its frame and counterweight beam, and beyond the water a large
  windmill (stellingmolen) and a church spire, both fading into the fog.
- Strong atmospheric perspective: every layer farther away is paler, bluer and lower in contrast;
  the sky is a flat pale grey haze. Silhouettes, not detail, carry the far planes.
- Mood: quiet, eerie, cinematic, very restrained colour. Amber windows and the small red figure
  are the only saturated notes.

## B. The zombie canal at night (the playful, detailed version)

The same world at full night, richer and more colourful, warm against cold.

- Deep navy night; warm amber street lamps (classic lanterns) throw visible light cones and pools
  on wet cobbles; lit windows are amber with small details (a tulip in a pot, a cat silhouette).
- Centre foreground, seen from behind: the hero in an orange-red jacket holding a flashlight whose
  cone lights the street ahead.
- Left: a lurching zombie with an orange bucket hat, glowing yellow eyes, a ragged shirt, red and
  white striped socks and wooden clogs; a bicycle leaning on a lamppost; a planter of red tulips.
- Midground: more zombies shambling along the street and over an arched canal bridge with iron
  railings; a big windmill with lit windows dominates the skyline, church spires in the haze; a
  Dutch flag (red, white, blue) hangs from a house.
- Right: the canal with a moored wooden boat, a cart stacked with yellow cheese wheels, a zombie
  wearing a huge cheese wheel as a hat, and a white duck in the water with glowing eyes.
  Reflections of lamps and windows shimmer on the canal.
- Tone: Halloween-dark but funny, full of Dutch cliches done with love. Lots of small details.

## C. The concept sheet (shapes, cast and props)

A sheet with labelled panels on a dark slate background, all in the same restrained palette.

- Hero street scene: the small figure in red walking past lit houses, zombies under the lamps,
  an arched bridge over the canal, a windmill behind.
- ZOMBIE SILHOUETTE: a tall, thin, hunched figure with very long dangling arms, a small head pushed
  forward, glowing yellow-orange eyes. A walk cycle of five silhouettes, each more hunched, the
  last one almost crawling.
- ZOMBIE VARIATIONS: one in a bucket hat; a woman in traditional dress with a white Dutch bonnet
  and a long dark skirt; a plain hunched one; one riding a bicycle; one carrying a tall stack of
  cheese or boxes on his back.
- DUTCH ELEMENTS (SCENES): a windmill in fog; a red tulip field with a church behind; a drawbridge;
  a row of gabled canal houses with lit windows; a fishing boat at a quay.
- BICYCLES: a classic black omafiets, a bakfiets (cargo bike with a wooden box), a bike lying on
  the ground.
- CHEESE: cheese wheels on a wooden cart, a cheese shop with a hanging cheese sign, stacked wheels
  glowing warm yellow.
- CLOGS (KLOMPEN): a pair of yellow wooden clogs, a clog shop sign.
- ENVIRONMENT PROPS: an ornate street lantern with warm light, black Amsterdammertje bollards, a
  park bench, a blue round bike-lane sign, a wooden fingerpost pointing to "Dorp" and "Molen", a
  rubbish bin, a tulip planter.
- ARCHITECTURE: narrow canal houses with stepped and bell gables and warm windows, next to a
  windmill.
- INTERACTIONS / GAMEPLAY IDEAS: hide behind a crate while zombies pass, distract them by throwing
  something, use a cheese cart to lure them, a thrown clog makes a noise that draws them away.
- Palette across the sheet: desaturated navy and slate blues and greys, warm amber lamps and
  windows, muted oranges, red tulips, yellow cheese, glowing yellow eyes.

## D. The foggy village with the dead (A plus menace)

Composition A, now inhabited.

- A hunched zombie peers over the garden fence next to the lit window, eyes glowing.
- Zombies shamble along the street under the lamps, whose light cones are visible in the fog.
- Small figures cross the drawbridge; lamp light is reflected as long streaks on the wet road.
- A bicycle leans against the fence; the windmill and the town still fade into the haze.

## What to take from all four

- Fog and atmospheric perspective do most of the work; detail lives in the near planes.
- Amber light is the warmth; orange is the hero; yellow eyes are the threat; everything else is
  blue-grey.
- Wet ground everywhere, with reflections and light streaks.
- The cast: a small hero seen mostly from behind, tall thin hunched zombies with Dutch cliches worn
  as costume (bucket hat, bonnet, clogs, striped socks, cheese), a white duck, a cat in a window.
- The props: drawbridges, arched bridges with railings, windmills, gabled houses, canal boats,
  cheese carts, tulip boxes, bollards, fingerposts, bikes everywhere.
